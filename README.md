🚀 Excited to share my latest End-to-End Data Analytics Project: Pizza Sales Performance Optimization! 🍕📈

In this project, I acted as a Data Analyst & BI Developer to transform raw transactional sales data into actionable, executive-level insights that drive data-backed business decisions.

🛠️ The Modern Tech Stack & Architecture:
1️⃣ Data Engineering Layer (SQL Server): Designed and optimized 17 database Views using T-SQL to orchestrate raw data extraction, compute transactional KPIs, and build high-performance metric rankings (Best/Worst sellers).
2️⃣ Business Intelligence Layer (Power BI Desktop): Developed an interactive dynamic dashboard using the modern Fluent 2 design language, built solid relationships in the Data Model, and extracted deeper insights.

💡 Key Analytical Findings (2015 Fiscal Year):
• Macro Operations: Total Revenue reached $817.86K across 21,350 distinct orders, with Friday, Thursday, and Saturday identified as peak velocity windows.
• Product Insights: Large (L) size pizzas dominated consumer preferences, driving 45.89% of global sales.
• Menu Performance: While "The Thai Chicken Pizza" was the absolute revenue champion ($43.43K), "The Brie Carre Pizza" severely underperformed across all dimensions, leading to a direct operational recommendation for menu rationalization.

🎯 Strategic Business Recommendations Delivered:
• Dynamic workforce scheduling aligned with weekend operational demand spikes.
• Proactive supply chain scaling ahead of seasonal summer surges (May–August).
• Immediate lifecycle interventions for lowest-performing SKUs to eliminate inventory carrying costs.
